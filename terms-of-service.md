## 3. Allergen and dietary information — important limitations

**Please read this section carefully if you have food allergies or strict dietary requirements.**

Tashika uses AI to identify and flag potential allergens and dietary information based on dish names, menu descriptions, and visible ingredient lists. This process has significant limitations:

- AI-generated allergen information may be incomplete, inaccurate, or based on typical rather than actual ingredients
- Restaurant recipes and ingredient sourcing vary between establishments and change without notice
- Cross-contamination risks cannot be detected or assessed by Tashika
- Hidden or unlisted ingredients cannot be identified from a menu scan alone
- Translation accuracy depends on AI interpretation and is not guaranteed

**If you have a severe food allergy or a medically required dietary restriction, you must always:**

1. Verify allergen information directly with restaurant staff before ordering
2. Not rely solely on Tashika's output as confirmation that a dish is safe for you
3. Use the app's hold-to-show feature to communicate your specific needs to staff
4. Carry any necessary medication (such as an EpiPen) regardless of app output

Ash Every Design Office accepts no liability for adverse health outcomes, allergic reactions, or dietary violations resulting from reliance on information provided by Tashika. Use of Tashika for allergen or dietary decision-making is entirely at your own risk.
